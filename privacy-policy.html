TEDUH APDL Fast Access — Privacy Policy

This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

All data (recent APDL lookups and cached unit counts) is stored locally on your device using Chrome's storage API.

The extension communicates only with teduh.kpkt.gov.my to fetch publicly available project and unit data through its public REST APIs.

No analytics, tracking, or third-party services are used.
